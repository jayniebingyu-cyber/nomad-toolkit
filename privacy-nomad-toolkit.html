1. Who we are

Nomad Toolkit ("we", "our", "the company") sells practical digital guides (PDF downloads) and publishes related content on third-party platforms, including Pinterest. This policy explains what data we handle, and what we do with it.

2. What data we collect

We do not operate user accounts and do not collect personal data on this website.

  • Purchases: handled entirely by Gumroad, Inc. Gumroad collects buyer name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy (gumroad.com/privacy). We receive order confirmations only.
  • This website: hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs (IP address, user agent) as described in the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Newsletter/email: if you contact us at the address above, we receive your email address and message content.

3. Pinterest API usage

We operate an internal application ("Nomad Toolkit Publisher") that connects to the Pinterest API solely for our own business account:

  • It creates and publishes Pins promoting our own digital products to our own boards.
  • It reads only our own boards, Pins and basic account analytics needed to operate those Pins.
  • It does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, does not scrape content, and does not provide analytics or advertising services to third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored on our own secured server and never shared.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We never use Pinterest user data for advertising profiles.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

5. Data retention & security

Order records are retained in Gumroad under their policies. API credentials are stored with restricted file permissions on a private server. You may request deletion of any data we hold about you by emailing niebingyu@qq.com.
